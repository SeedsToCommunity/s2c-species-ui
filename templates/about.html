Seeds to Community

Connecting people with native plants for ecological restoration in Southeast Michigan.

Our Mission

Seeds to Community is a collaborative project of the Stewardship Network dedicated to expanding knowledge and access to native plant seeds in Southeast Michigan. We believe that by sharing information about seed collection, processing, and growing, we can help more people participate in ecological restoration.

This Plant Species Directory is a living resource built by and for our community. It contains practical information about native plants, including when to collect seeds, how to process them, and what conditions they need to thrive.

Get Involved

Whether you're an experienced botanist or just beginning to learn about native plants, there's a place for you in our community. Join us to learn, share knowledge, and help restore native ecosystems across Southeast Michigan.
